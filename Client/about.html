About This Application

This application helps video game fans organize and keep track of their favorite video games in one place.

It can be used by gamers who want an easy way to browse their collection, learn about different games, and keep track of the games they own or enjoy playing.

The application manages a collection of video games. Each game can include information such as its name, genre, description, and other details about the game.
